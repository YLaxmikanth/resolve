# ResolveAI

### Autonomous Customer Resolution Platform

ResolveAI is an AI-assisted customer support resolution platform that analyzes customer complaints, investigates simulated business data, applies deterministic business policies, executes controlled actions, and verifies the outcome.

The system combines **Google Gemini** for natural-language understanding with a **deterministic resolution engine** for reliable business decisions.
